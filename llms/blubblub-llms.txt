# Speech Blubs

> Speech Blubs is a speech therapy app for children aged 1–8 that uses video modeling, speech exercises, and play-based activities to help kids with speech delays, autism, Down syndrome, apraxia, and other speech and language challenges. Used by over 7 million families worldwide and recommended by speech-language pathologists.

Speech Blubs was created by a team of speech-language pathologists and child development experts. The app is available on iOS and Android and supports English, Spanish, and German.
